Add another device to this account. Each device keeps its own keys; a cross-signed device roster is published to the transparency log, so no one can add a device without your key. Revoking a device cuts it off immediately.

App lock

Quick unlock wraps your vault key with a key held in this device's OS key store — it never weakens the password itself. 5 wrong PINs disable PIN unlock until you sign in with the password. Fingerprint unlock and auto-unlock are mutually exclusive; the PIN can stay on alongside either.

Privacy

With message requests on, someone new has to knock first — you decide who reaches you, and "message with request" controls whether their first message travels along or stays back until you accept. Typing and read receipts are courtesy signals — turn either off and this device sends nothing (it never sends-then-hides). Notification content controls how much a lock screen reveals when a message arrives.

Notifications & delivery

Connection mode keeps an encrypted socket open (no third party, more battery). Push mode lets the relay send a content-free wake-up — through the system push, or on de-Googled phones through a UnifiedPush app of your choice — when a message waits. Nothing readable ever leaves this device.

Updates

Updates come from your relay operator's channel and are signature-verified on this device before anything installs.

Account

Account changes take your password, an OS identity check (on Android), and your PIN — all three, in that order. Deleting the account is the same ceremony and can only be done from the primary device: the relay forgets you, your username frees up after 7 days, other devices are unlinked, and this device is wiped.
